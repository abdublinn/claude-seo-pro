# E-E-A-T Evaluation Framework — 2026 Reference

Based on Google Search Quality Rater Guidelines (updated Sept 2025).

## Overview

E-E-A-T applies to ALL competitive queries (expanded from YMYL in Dec 2025 update).

| Signal | Weight | Description |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| **Experience** | 25% | First-hand experience with the topic |
| **Expertise** | 25% | Knowledge and skill in the subject area |
| **Authoritativeness** | 25% | Recognized authority in the field |
| **Trustworthiness** | 25% | Accuracy, transparency, safety |

## Experience Signals

### Strong
- Original photos/videos (not stock)
- First-person case studies
- Process documentation ("here's how we make our product")
- Customer stories with verifiable details
- Author has demonstrable experience (bio, portfolio)

### Weak
- Only stock photos
- Generic "about us" with no specifics
- No author bylines
- No customer testimonials
- Content reads as AI-generated without human review

## Expertise Signals

### Strong
- Technical depth appropriate to topic complexity
- Correct industry terminology
- Cited sources (studies, standards, regulations)
- Author credentials displayed
- Content reviewed by qualified expert (for YMYL topics)

### Weak
- Surface-level content ("tips and tricks" without depth)
- Incorrect terminology or outdated information
- No sources cited
- Anonymous content
- Unrealistic claims without evidence ("0% defect rate", "best in market")

## Authoritativeness Signals

### Strong
- Known brand in the niche (search for brand name → results)
- Quality backlink profile
- Mentioned in authoritative publications
- Industry certifications/awards displayed
- Active presence on industry platforms
- Wikipedia/Wikidata entry

### Weak
- No brand recognition
- No external mentions
- No certifications
- New domain with no history

## Trustworthiness Signals

### Strong
- Complete contact information (address, phone, email)
- Privacy policy and terms of service
- Secure site (HTTPS, security headers)
- Clear return/refund policy (e-commerce)
- Verified reviews from multiple platforms
- Transparent pricing
- Honest about limitations

### Weak
- Missing contact info
- No privacy policy
- HTTP-only
- Hidden pricing
- Fake reviews or no reviews
- Unrealistic promises
- No physical address for local business

## Red Flags (automatic trust penalty)

- Unverifiable superlatives: "best", "cheapest", "#1" without evidence
- "0% defect rate" or similar impossible claims
- Fake reviews (identical language, suspicious patterns)
- Missing required legal pages
- No way to contact the business
- Content contradicts well-established facts
