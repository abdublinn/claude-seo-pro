# Agency SEO Plan Template

## Key Priorities
1. Service page optimization (one page per core service)
2. Portfolio/case study pages with results
3. Local SEO (if serving local market)
4. Team expertise pages (E-E-A-T)
5. Client testimonials with schema

## Content Strategy
- Industry-specific service pages
- Case studies with measurable results
- Thought leadership blog
- Tool/resource pages
- Comparison content (agency vs in-house vs freelancer)
- Free audit/assessment landing pages

## Schema Priorities
1. Organization (homepage)
2. Service (each service page)
3. Article (blog posts)
4. Person (team members)
5. Review/AggregateRating (testimonials)
