# Local Service Business SEO Plan Template

## Key Priorities
1. Google Business Profile optimization
2. LocalBusiness schema with correct subtype
3. NAP consistency across all directories
4. Review generation strategy
5. Location/service area pages

## Common Local Issues
- Inconsistent NAP (name/address/phone) across directories
- Missing or incomplete GBP listing
- No local schema or wrong subtype
- No embedded Google Map
- Reviews only on one platform

## Content Strategy
- Service area pages (city + service combination)
- Case studies / project showcases
- FAQ pages for common local questions
- Team/about page with real photos
- Community involvement / local news
